# Latana

> Latana is a Berlin-based brand tracking and consumer-insights platform. It measures brand
> awareness, perception, consideration and ad awareness by collecting non-incentivized
> micro-surveys through advertising placements rather than incentivized survey panels, then
> applying Bayesian modelling and machine-learning quality assurance to project results onto
> real-world populations. Customers include Unilever, Amazon, IKEA, Uber, PVH and Emma Sleep.
> Latana is backed by Balderton Capital.

## Status

- No public API. Latana publishes no developer portal, API reference, OpenAPI description,
  SDKs, CLI or public API host as of 2026-07-19. Delivery is through the Latana dashboard at
  app.latana.com plus dashboard data export; a Latana Slack app is the only announced
  programmatic surface.
